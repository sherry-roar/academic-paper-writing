# Final Checks

Select the checks that apply to the requested edit. Use the manuscript for the contribution narrative and accompanying notes for verification status or unresolved questions.

## Narrative

- Does the paper revolve around its strongest supported advantage?
- Do the problem, gap, insight, method, principal result, and conclusion form one argument?
- Is the strongest number or trend stated explicitly in the prose?
- Does each experiment or analysis have a role in that argument?
- Are alternatives presented as design-space choices rather than a development diary?
- Are less favorable comparisons explained through supported objectives or trade-offs?
- Have self-weakening phrases, repeated caveats, and unsolicited weakness summaries been removed?
- Does the conclusion reinforce the memorable contribution?

## Facts and evidence

- Are numbers, units, equations, names, definitions, and measurement boundaries unchanged unless a correction was authorized?
- Do claims use the actual baseline, setting, and aggregation rule?
- Are measured, derived, simulated, and predicted quantities identified correctly?
- Are scientific properties supported by the appropriate evidence?
- Are citation claims and bibliography entries verified against available sources?
- Are missing evidence and conflicting sources recorded for the author rather than invented?

## English

- Are subjects, actions, comparisons, and pronoun references clear?
- Are grammar, articles, countability, parallelism, tense, and modifiers correct in context?
- Are technical terms consistent across the edited sections?
- Have generic praise and empty effectiveness statements been replaced with specifics?
- Are common conditions defined once at the appropriate location?
- Does newly written prose follow the requested house style without rewriting protected, approved text?

## Figures and tables

- Does each visual make its intended comparison clear?
- Are metrics, units, baselines, and normalization defined?
- Are identities, panel orders, and encodings consistent?
- Are failed or missing included cases distinguished from zero?
- Are captions concise and free of repeated setup or legend narration?
- Is the rendered figure or table readable at the actual publication size and in grayscale?
- Are plotting data and editable sources traceable when a figure was created or changed?

## File and venue work, when requested

- Were only authorized files edited?
- Are markup, equations, labels, citations, and cross-references intact?
- Was the relevant document build or rendering inspected when available?
- Were target requirements checked against the current official source?
- Are checks performed, checks unavailable, and concrete author decisions distinguished in the delivery notes?
- Have working placeholders been resolved before calling a document ready for submission?

Do not describe an excerpt edit as a complete scientific or submission audit. Deliver the requested writing result and a proportionate account of the work actually done.
