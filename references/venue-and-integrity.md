# Venue Requirements, Citations, and Factual Integrity

Use this module for citation work and requested journal or conference preparation. It supplies a verification process; it does not embed the requirements of a particular venue.

## Verify the actual target

Identify the journal or conference, article type or track, and applicable submission round or edition when these affect the requested work. Read the current official author instructions and the official template or call as relevant.

Record the source URL and access date in working notes. Separate verified requirements from author preferences and practices observed in published papers. When official material is unavailable or ambiguous, mark the specific point unverified instead of inventing a rule.

Possible items to check, as relevant to the task:

- Template, document class, class options, page size, and active build entrypoint.
- Page or word limits and what counts toward them.
- Abstract format, keywords, highlights, and required section headings.
- Anonymous review, author details, acknowledgments, and self-citation treatment.
- Figures, tables, fonts, file formats, accessibility, and production requirements.
- References, appendices, supplementary files, artifacts, and availability statements.
- Prior-publication or extended-version disclosure.
- Required ethics, funding, conflicts, data, and writing-assistance statements.

Not every item applies to every paper. Do not add an unrequested disclosure section or administrative task on the basis of a hypothetical rule.

## Check the real rendered artifact

Verify that the intended template options are actually active. A class option written in a file may be overridden by another entrypoint or build configuration.

Assess page limits in the actual required layout. A page count from another paper size or template is not interchangeable. Check figures and tables at the final physical size rather than relying on enlarged previews.

A successful build is evidence that the document compiled; it does not establish scientific validity, complete compliance, or readiness for submission. Describe the checks actually performed in the accompanying notes.

## Verify citations

Use supplied references and verified source material. Check that the cited work supports the statement being made, and preserve the distinction between its actual result and the manuscript's interpretation.

Verify author names, title, year, venue, identifiers, version, and bibliography key before changing an entry. Do not fabricate a DOI or infer a full result from a title alone. Prefer authoritative publisher or author sources for bibliographic verification.

If a citation is needed but its source is not available, use a clearly marked working placeholder or an issue note. Do not create a plausible-looking reference. Remove or resolve placeholders before final submission preparation.

Do not copy distinctive text from a reference paper into the manuscript. A stylistic model supplies rhetorical methods, not text or scientific evidence for a different study.

## Preserve the factual layer

Keep values, units, formulas, algorithms, configurations, and evidence identities faithful to the actual records. Distinguish measurements, calculations, simulations, model estimates, predictions, and failed cases in those records.

Do not extend a numerical diagnostic into a claim about every scientific property, a build into a claim of correct execution, or a single timing into a claim of stable statistics. Use the supported factual statement and organize it according to the launch-presentation principles.

Correct a known factual error rather than improving its wording. If sources conflict, record the discrepancy and seek the authoritative evidence; do not select the convenient value.

## Keep manuscript and working notes distinct

The manuscript makes the contribution persuasive. Working notes hold verification status, missing sources, operational records, and unresolved author decisions. Do not automatically turn those notes into defensive manuscript prose.

When a verified venue requirement or an explicit author request calls for specific scope or limitation content, supply it at the required location. Otherwise, follow the skill's original narrative approach: conditions defined once, results stated directly, and supported trade-offs explained through design objectives.

## Submission boundary

Preparing files does not authorize uploading them, contacting an editor, sending a response, changing authorship, or submitting a paper. Those actions require the author's instruction. Do not manufacture a submission status, acceptance status, or prior-review history.
