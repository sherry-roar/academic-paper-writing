# Working Templates

Use these lightweight templates only when they make the current task easier. They are working artifacts, not mandatory manuscript sections. Replace illustrative fields with verified information and keep unresolved questions out of finished prose.

## Main-line card

```text
Research object:
Concrete problem:
What existing work establishes:
What remains unresolved:
Central insight:
Strongest supported advantage:
Most informative result and comparison:
Method choices that create that advantage:
Where common conditions are defined:
Conclusion's memorable message:
```

## Contribution-to-evidence map

| Contribution | Method or reasoning | Evidence location | Role in the paper |
|---|---|---|---|
| The specific increment | How the increment is achieved | Verified figure, table, derivation, or source | Why the reader needs it |

Use this map to align the abstract, introduction, method, evaluation, and conclusion. Keep unresolved evidence in a separate note rather than filling a cell with invented support.

## Section-function map

| Section | Reader's question | Main message | Needed evidence or definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction | Why this study? | The problem and central contribution | Prior work and principal result |
| Method | How does the idea work? | The representation, reasoning, or procedure | Definitions and design rationale |
| Evaluation | What does it establish? | The strongest supported finding | Relevant comparisons and analysis |
| Conclusion | What should the reader remember? | The contribution and result | Evidence already presented |

Adapt the rows to the actual article type. Preserve an effective existing structure.

## Experiment role card

```text
Research question:
Comparison and conditions:
Metric and measurement boundary:
Strongest result or trend:
Mechanism evidence:
Supported objective/trade-off explanation:
Main-text role:
Supporting-record location:
```

An experiment without an argumentative role need not become a main-text subsection.

## Paragraph planning card

```text
What this paragraph establishes:
Evidence or reasoning:
Connection to the central contribution:
What the next paragraph needs from it:
```

Use this when logic is tangled. Do not force every sentence into a fixed formula.

## Editing ledger

| Location | Change | Purpose | Factual content preserved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Section, paragraph, or label | Concrete wording or organization change | Clarity, logic, emphasis, or consistency | Numbers, terms, equations, and comparison conditions checked |

For a short edit, a few sentences can replace the table.

## Working issue note

```text
Unresolved item:
Relevant source or location:
What is known:
What evidence or author decision is needed:
Which requested edit depends on it:
Independent edits already completed:
```

This note is for the author. Do not append it to the manuscript as a blanket disclaimer.

## Venue check record

| Item relevant to this task | Official source and access date | Verified requirement | Applied check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Format, page limit, anonymity, or another actual requirement | Official URL and date | Exact applicable rule | What was inspected or changed |

Record unavailable information as unverified. Do not copy a different venue's rule into the requirement column.
